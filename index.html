Agent safety · Human-AI coevolution · Agent evaluation

Hi! I recently graduated from Northeastern University in Boston, MA, and will be joining Chats Lab as a PhD student in Fall 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Weiyan Shi. In Summer 2026, I interned at VirtueAI and Meta Superintelligence Labs, working with Prof. Bo Li and Minzhou Pan on agent safety and agent attacks.

Broadly, I want to understand how humans can stay in the loop and use AI well. More specifically:

  • Agent safety — what goes wrong when the human is not actively in the loop, and how tool-using agents drift unsafe over long interactions.
  • Human-AI coevolution — how people can evolve to use AI better, and how AI reshapes them in return.
  • Agent evaluation — what agents can really do, how they do it, and which parts genuinely assist humans.

I'm always happy to chat about these topics — feel free to reach out.
